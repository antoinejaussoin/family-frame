# Company and consumer law

The legal shell around the product: entity, terms, warranty, tax,
insurance.

Plan stages: in place for **3**, complete for **5**. See
[`plan.md`](plan.md).

> Checklist, not legal or tax advice. Confirm with an accountant and a
> solicitor.

## Where we are

No company. One personal project. Code public under GPL and AGPL
([`licensing.md`](licensing.md)).

## Entity

Incorporate before the first unit is sold or lent to a stranger.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| UK private limited company | Limits personal liability for a product with a battery on a kitchen wall; easier to insure and raise money | Accounts and filings each year |
| Sole trader | Simplest | Personal liability for product claims |

Recommendation: a limited company before Stage 3 sales.

## Consumer law (UK)

- **Consumer Rights Act 2015.** Goods must be of satisfactory quality,
  fit for purpose, and as described. 30-day right to reject; repair or
  replacement after that; within six months, faults are presumed present
  at delivery. The cloud service counts as a digital service under the
  same Act.
- **Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.** 14-day cancellation for
  online sales, plus pre-contract information.
- **Subscriptions.** If the Cloud frame has a subscription, check the
  subscription-contract rules under the Digital Markets, Competition and
  Consumers Act 2024 (renewal reminders, easy cancellation) and their
  commencement dates.
- **Advertising claims.** “Six months on a charge” must be substantiated
  ([`offer.md`](offer.md)).

Selling into France adds the EU legal guarantee of conformity (two years)
and French consumer-code requirements.

## Tax

- UK VAT registration once taxable turnover passes the threshold
  (£90 000 at the time of writing), or voluntarily earlier.
- Selling goods from the UK to EU consumers: import VAT and duties apply
  at the border. Decide between delivered duty paid, an EU fulfilment
  partner, or the Import One-Stop Shop (only for consignments up to
  €150, which a frame exceeds).
- Cloud subscriptions to EU consumers: VAT in the customer’s country
  through the One-Stop Shop for digital services.

## Contracts and policies

- Terms of sale (hardware) and terms of service (cloud).
- Warranty statement on top of statutory rights.
- Returns and refunds process.
- Privacy notice ([`privacy.md`](privacy.md)).
- Pilot agreement for Stage 3 units if lent.
- Vulnerability disclosure policy ([`certification.md`](certification.md)).
- End-of-life commitment: what happens to the Cloud frame if the service
  closes (data export and a route to a Home frame), and that the Home
  frame keeps working without us.

## Insurance

Product liability, public liability, and cyber cover before Stage 3
sales. Professional indemnity if offering setup services.

## Trade mark and domain

Check “Family Frame” availability in the UK and EU before spending on
branding. It is a descriptive phrase and may be hard to register; have a
fallback name.
