# Go-to-market

Who buys first, how they hear about it, and how the product grows into
an international widget board.

Plan stages: pilots in **3**, shop open in **5**, wider channels in
**6**. See [`plan.md`](plan.md).

## Where we are

A public GitHub repository with a product-style README, screenshots, and
a build guide ([`README.md`](../README.md)). No shop, no waitlist, no
audience measurement.

## Decided

- **2026-10-10** The product becomes country-agnostic with country packs;
  the long-term market is international ([`widgets.md`](widgets.md)).

## First buyers (Stage 3)

People who already want this and forgive rough edges:

- Families who read the README and would otherwise build one.
- Home Assistant and self-hosting households, who value the Home frame’s
  local-only design.
- E-ink enthusiasts who want a 13.3″ colour panel without soldering.
- Friends of friends in the UK and France who match the original
  household’s shape.

What we learn from them matters more than revenue: where setup breaks,
real battery life, which widgets get used.

## Positioning

What sets this apart, all checkable today:

- Colour e-ink: no glow, readable in daylight, picture stays with the
  power off.
- Months between charges, no cable on the wall.
- A choice of local-only (Home frame) or no extra box (Cloud frame).
- A kitchen board first, photo frame second.

## Channels

| Channel | Stage | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist on a landing page | 2–3 | Measures demand before buying panels |
| Build log and README | 3 | Existing audience; depends on repo visibility ([`licensing.md`](licensing.md)) |
| Home Assistant, e-ink, and self-hosting communities | 3 | Where the first buyers already are |
| Crowdfunding (for example Crowd Supply or Kickstarter) | 5 | Funds the first batch; Crowd Supply suits open hardware and handles fulfilment |
| Own shop | 5 | Full margin; we handle VAT, shipping, returns |
| Parenting and home media, reviewers | 5 | Once setup no longer needs us |
| Retailers and marketplaces | 6 | Needs margin for the retailer and volume supply |

## Going international

Each new country needs: widget packs (transit, waste, school), translated
interface and widget text, local date and calendar conventions, shipping
and VAT setup, and compliance registrations
([`certification.md`](certification.md)). Pick countries where the
first two are cheap and demand shows up on the waitlist.
