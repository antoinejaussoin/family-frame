Bin / recycling day — next collection and which stream (rubbish, recycling, garden). This is the classic “why do we have a kitchen board” item. Councils usually publish an ICS or a scrapeable calendar.

Birthdays & anniversaries — next 2–4 from a dedicated calendar, as a short “soon” strip. A countdown (“Maya 7 on Thursday”) lands better than another generic event row.

School term / inset / holidays — inset days and half-term wreck the week if they are buried in Coming next. A one-line “term ends 18 Oct” or “inset Friday” is enough.

Rain chance or “take a coat” — you already have icons and temps; a % rain or a single yellow “wet commute” flag for the next school-run slot is more useful than a third forecast day.

Sunset — changes slowly, so it will not burn the panel. Helps with after-school outdoor time and “when do we need lights on”.

Travel / line status — only if you actually use a line or a school bus. Hourly is fine for “Elizabeth line part-suspended”, useless for live bus minutes.


Frame battery — the Pico already has battery code; a discreet “frame 62%” in the mast avoids a dead panel surprise.

Humidity / mould warning — you already have humidity. Only highlight rooms over a threshold (yellow/red) instead of adding more numbers.
