1. Term, inset, half-term
One line: “inset Friday” or “term ends 18 Oct.” Buried in Coming next they get missed; on their own they save the week.

2. Kit flags (PE, swimming, packed lunch)
Weekday-only, pulled from the calendar or a tiny list. “PE: both · swimming bag” is the reason a kitchen board exists.

3. Tonight’s dinner / the week’s meals
Mealime, Paprika, a shared sheet, or a note you type in the family UI. The most-looked-at surface in the house is the fridge door.

4. School canteen menu
Many UK and French schools publish a weekly PDF or page. “Today: jacket potato” decides lunchboxes before 8am.

5. After-school clubs and sports fixtures
Not another calendar row — a “who is where after 3:30” strip. U10s Saturday 10:00 away belongs here, not in Coming next with the dentist.

6. Who’s in / who’s out
Home Assistant, phone-on-Wi‑Fi, or calendar “office / WFH.” Morning wake only: “Maya at school · Dad office.” Skip live tracking; that’s a phone job.

7. Exception banner
Rail strike, school snow day, severe TfL, bin collection moved. Hide the row when life is normal. Highest value-per-pixel of anything you could add.

8. Cheap-energy window
Octopus Agile / Home Assistant: “dishwasher after 2pm.” Changes slowly, kitchen-native, and you already have rooms on the board.

9. Machine finished
Washer / dryer / dishwasher as a single exception, not a device list. “Laundry done” at the evening wake is enough.

10. Coat / sun-cream / pollen
You already have icons and temps. A yellow flag for the next school-run slot (“wet · 70%”) or summer UV/pollen beats a third forecast day. Same idea as the rain note in ideas.md.

11. Countdown to the next family thing
“12 days to half-term.” “Granny Thursday.” One number, almost never refreshes the glass.

12. Expected deliveries
A one-line “DPD 10–12” from a tracking feed or a parsed shipping mail. Classic hallway question.

13. Library / passport / MOT / prescription
Slow admin with a real penalty if you miss it. “Library due Sat” or “MOT in 18 days.” Perfect for e-ink.

14. Chore / table rota
“Tuesday: Maya sets table · Leo dishwasher.” Static all day, changes once overnight.

15. Bank holiday / jour férié
With Pronote + saints you already lean French; “shops shut Monday” is more useful than another saint line. Same slot as inset days.

16. Guest Wi‑Fi QR
Never changes, so the checksum stays quiet. Lives in a corner; visitors stop asking for the password.

Honourable mentions: sunset / “lights on” (already in ideas.md), grandparents’ timezone (“good time to call after 4”), school-run bus/train for your stop rather than Tube status, a pinned family note from the UI (“piano tuner Thursday”).

What I’d skip: now-playing, live scores, chat digests, screen-time dashboards. They go stale between wakes and they aren’t glanceable from the kettle.
